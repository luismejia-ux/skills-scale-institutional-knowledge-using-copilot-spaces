# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate and oversee the deployment of software releases from development through production. They ensure smooth deployments, manage release schedules, and coordinate across teams to minimize risk and downtime.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule releases in coordination with Product and Engineering
- Coordinate deployment activities across multiple teams and environments
- Manage release documentation including release notes and deployment runbooks
- Monitor deployments and coordinate rollback procedures if needed
- Ensure compliance with change management processes
- Track release metrics (deployment frequency, lead time, failure rate)

### Goals
- Achieve zero-downtime deployments
- Reduce deployment risk through standardized processes
- Improve release frequency and reliability
- Maintain clear audit trails for all releases

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with PM and Engineering leads
- Go/No-Go meetings before major deployments
- Release status updates to stakeholders
- Post-deployment reports and metrics reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Coordinate feature completion and deployment readiness
- **Project Managers**: Align release schedules with project timelines
- **Security Engineer**: Ensure security reviews are complete before release
- **QA/Testing**: Validate release candidates and sign-off on quality gates

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers research user needs, design intuitive interfaces, and validate solutions through user testing. They ensure products are usable, accessible, and deliver excellent user experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs
- Establish and maintain design systems and UI patterns
- Collaborate with Product and Engineering on feature design
- Advocate for accessibility and inclusive design practices
- Validate designs through user feedback and analytics

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and improve satisfaction scores
- Maintain design consistency across products
- Ensure designs are technically feasible and performant

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions with Product and Engineering
- User research presentations and findings
- Design specs and handoff documentation
- Feedback sessions on prototypes and implementations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on feature definition and user journeys
- **Developers**: Provide design specs and partner on implementation
- **Customer Success Lead**: Gather user feedback and pain points
- **Data Analyst**: Review usage analytics to inform design decisions

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts collect, analyze, and interpret data to inform product decisions and measure success. They provide insights that drive strategy and help teams understand user behavior and business impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Build dashboards and reports for stakeholders
- Conduct data analysis to answer business questions
- Identify trends, patterns, and opportunities in data
- Ensure data quality and integrity
- Collaborate with teams to instrument analytics properly

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision making
- Provide actionable insights that improve products and processes
- Ensure accurate and timely reporting
- Build self-service analytics capabilities

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metrics reviews with Product and Engineering
- Ad-hoc data requests and analysis presentations
- Documentation of KPIs and measurement plans
- Data quality reports and recommendations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Define success metrics and analyze feature impact
- **Developers**: Collaborate on instrumentation and data collection
- **UX Designer**: Share usage analytics to inform design decisions
- **Stakeholder Advisor**: Present business metrics and insights

---

## Customer Success Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success Leads ensure customers achieve their goals using our products. They gather customer feedback, advocate for customer needs internally, and coordinate support escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Build relationships with key customers and stakeholders
- Gather and synthesize customer feedback and feature requests
- Coordinate escalations and high-priority support issues
- Track customer satisfaction and health metrics
- Provide product education and training
- Represent customer voice in product planning

### Goals
- Improve customer retention and satisfaction (NPS, CSAT)
- Reduce time to value for new customers
- Increase product adoption and feature usage
- Build strong customer relationships and advocacy

### Typical Communication
- Regular customer check-ins and business reviews
- Internal updates on customer feedback and trends
- Escalation coordination with Engineering and Support
- Feature request prioritization discussions with Product

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Share customer feedback and feature requests
- **UX Designer**: Provide user insights and usability feedback
- **Developers**: Coordinate on bug fixes and customer escalations
- **Stakeholder Advisor**: Align on customer relationship strategies

---

## Security Engineer

### Role Summary
Security Engineers ensure the security, privacy, and compliance of our products and infrastructure. They identify vulnerabilities, implement security controls, and guide teams on security best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security reviews and threat modeling
- Perform vulnerability assessments and penetration testing
- Implement security controls and monitoring
- Review code for security issues
- Respond to security incidents
- Provide security guidance and training to teams
- Ensure compliance with security standards and regulations

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data leaks
- Maintain compliance with security standards (SOC2, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Build security into the development lifecycle (shift-left)
- Reduce time to detect and respond to security issues

### Typical Communication
- Security review sessions during project planning
- Vulnerability reports and remediation recommendations
- Security incident communications
- Security training and awareness sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Review code and provide security guidance
- **Release Manager**: Approve releases based on security posture
- **Project Managers**: Advise on security risks and timelines
- **Stakeholder Advisor**: Report on security compliance and posture

---

## Stakeholder Advisor

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Advisors represent executive leadership, business units, or key partners. They provide strategic direction, approve major decisions, and ensure projects align with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic priorities and business objectives
- Approve project charters, budgets, and major decisions
- Remove organizational blockers and secure resources
- Review project progress and outcomes
- Provide business context and market insights
- Champion initiatives across the organization

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Align projects with strategic objectives
- Maintain visibility into high-priority initiatives
- Enable teams through resources and organizational support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly project reviews and steering committees
- Executive briefings and decision forums
- Strategic planning sessions
- Escalation handling and resource allocation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Review status, approve changes, remove blockers
- **Product Managers**: Align on strategy and roadmap priorities
- **Data Analyst**: Review business metrics and outcomes
- **Customer Success Lead**: Discuss customer relationships and satisfaction

## Role Interaction Matrix

The following matrix shows key collaboration patterns between roles:

| Role | Primary Collaborators | Escalation Path | Key Artifacts |
|------|----------------------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Developers** | PM, PdM, UX Designer, Security Engineer | PM → Tech Lead → Engineering Manager | Code, Tests, PRs, Technical Designs |
| **Product Managers** | PdM, Developers, UX Designer, Data Analyst | Product Lead → VP Product → Stakeholder Advisor | Product Specs, Roadmaps, Success Metrics |
| **Project Managers** | All roles | Product Lead → Stakeholder Advisor | Project Plans, Status Reports, Risk Registers |
| **Release Manager** | Developers, PM, Security Engineer | Engineering Manager → VP Engineering | Release Notes, Deployment Runbooks, Release Metrics |
| **UX Designer** | PdM, Developers, Customer Success Lead | Design Lead → VP Product | Wireframes, Prototypes, Design Specs, Research Reports |
| **Data Analyst** | PdM, Developers, UX Designer | Data Lead → VP Product | Dashboards, Analytics Reports, KPI Definitions |
| **Customer Success Lead** | PdM, UX Designer, Support | CS Manager → VP Customer Success | Customer Feedback, Escalations, Satisfaction Metrics |
| **Security Engineer** | Developers, Release Manager, PM | Security Manager → CISO | Security Reviews, Vulnerability Reports, Compliance Docs |
| **Stakeholder Advisor** | PM, PdM | Executive Leadership | Business Strategy, Budget Approvals, Executive Briefings |
